Our Story

Driving Excellence Across Industries

ASAFU Group of Companies is a premier Nigerian conglomerate dedicated to delivering top-tier services in General Contracting, Real Estate, Agriculture, and Logistics. Established with a vision to bridge the gap between quality and efficiency, we have grown into a trusted partner for individuals, businesses, and government agencies.

With our headquarters in Kaduna and operations spanning the nation, we pride ourselves on a workforce that values integrity, innovation, and sustainable development. From constructing state-of-the-art facilities to empowering the agricultural sector, ASAFU is building the future.
